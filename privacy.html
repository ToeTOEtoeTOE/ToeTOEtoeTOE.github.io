Maple Classic Mob Helper

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Maple Classic Mob Helper ("the app") is an unofficial companion app for MapleStory Classic Worlds. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nexon.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The game data the app shows (monsters, maps and items), which comes inside the app and is copied into the app's own storage the first time you open it. The monster, item and map pictures are built into the app too.
  • Your favourites, your 20 most recently viewed entries and your boss timers.
  • Your settings (theme, notification options, ad-free period, whether you bought "Remove ads"), and in the store version your ad-consent choice, which Google's consent tool keeps on the phone.

All of this stays on your phone. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

What leaves your phone

The app asks no website for its game data or its pictures: both come inside the app.

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): details of crashes and of problems the app recovered from, such as game data it could not read, a boss timer it could not set again or, in the store version, an ad, consent or purchase step that failed (which record or step, the error, the app version, device model and Android version), the start of each app session (Crashlytics counts crash-free sessions with it), a random per-installation identifier, and the normal information any web request carries, such as your IP address. No personal information is added on purpose. Google's privacy policy applies.

The store version also exchanges data with Google for ads and purchases, as described under "Advertising" and "Purchases" below. The personal version contains no ad, consent or billing code at all.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics beyond crash reports.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing with third parties apart from the ads in the store version (see "Advertising").
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

Advertising (store version only)

The version of the app distributed through Google Play (the "store version") shows ads from Google AdMob. The privately built "personal" version contains no advertising code at all.

  • Who serves the ads: Google (AdMob). Google's own policy explains how it uses data from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • What AdMob may collect: device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, interactions with ads (impressions, clicks), and diagnostic data about ad delivery.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Privacy options; the row appears whenever these options apply to you.
  • Optional video ads: watching an optional video ad pauses ads for 24 hours. While ads are shown, the app keeps one video loaded in advance so it can start at once. The app stores only the time the pause ends, on your device.

Purchases (store version only)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details; it stores only whether the purchase is active, on your device, and asks Google Play again at each start and whenever you return to the app, so a refund or a purchase on another device is reflected.

Notifications

If you start a boss timer, the app may ask to show notifications. Exact alarms ("Alarms & reminders") are optional too: the app never asks for them, and the Boss Timers screen links to the setting. Both can be turned off in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
