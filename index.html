Maple Classic Mob Helper

An unofficial companion app for MapleStory Classic Worlds, for Android.

Look up what a monster drops, where it spawns, what an item does and when a field boss is due again. The app has respawn timers with reminders, search for monsters, maps and items, and favourites, and it works offline: its game data comes built in.
